About Monthly Idol

Monthly Idol is an idol management simulation game developed and published by 608factory.

Players manage their own entertainment agency, recruit and train artists, produce albums, organize schedules, and develop successful idol groups.

The game offers a simulation experience where players make management decisions and guide their artists toward success.

Account and Sign-In

Monthly Idol supports account-related features that allow players to access supported game services. Google Sign-In may be used for authentication where available.
